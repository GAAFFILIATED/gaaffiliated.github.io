How rankings are validated

Every retained entry provides a published meet-result URL, competition date, athlete name, country, event and age category. Results are source-linked but the compilation is not a complete global results audit. GA assigns ages by calendar year of the performance minus athlete birth year. Wind greater than +2.0 m/s is excluded for applicable events. If wind was not published, it is shown as unreported rather than assumed legal. Electronic timing, distance, and event specifications must be checked during import. Separate throw weights or hurdle heights must not be ranked together. More countries, meets, events and years will be added as verifiable sources and redistribution permissions allow.

Submit an official performance

To propose an addition or correction, email ga.affiliated@gmail.com with the public official result URL, athlete name, birth year, event, mark, competition date and nation. Do not submit private documents or full dates of birth for minors.
