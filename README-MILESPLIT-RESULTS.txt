INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE CSV
rankings-data.csv is hosted alongside track-top-100.html.
Columns: athlete,athlete_id,gender,age,event,mark,meet,date,state,team,source_url,status,wind
Set status=verified only after checking age, athlete, event, mark and source URL.
Age must be exact individual age; source reports 7-8 or 15-16 without per-athlete
age are not sufficient. Meet end date is used for multi-day FSG meet.
State=FL means the meet took place in Florida, not necessarily athlete residency.
For sprint/hurdle/jumps wind values, marks above +2.0m/s are not ranked.
For mixed metric field marks, use numeric meters; track times use seconds or MM:SS.HH.
No DOB, address or personal information beyond publicly posted meet data.
